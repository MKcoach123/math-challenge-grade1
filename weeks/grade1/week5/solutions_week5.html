Problem 1
Which digits are missing from the picture?
Answer: 6 and 9
Let’s write down all digits from the picture. We will get: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8. This means we are missing digits 6 and 9
Problem 2
Anusha is reading a book. On the first day she reads 10 pages. On the second day she reads 4 more pages than the first day. How many pages did Anusha read on the second day?
Answer: 14
First day: 10 pages; Second day: 10 pages + 4 pages = 14 pages

Problem 4
The same number is hiding behind each butterfly. What number is it?
Answer: 4
Let’s make a guess that behind a butterfly a number 2 is missing. 2 + 2 + 2 = 6, which is less than 12. Let’s try 3: 3 + 3 + 3 = 9, still not enough. Let’s try 4: 4 + 4 + 4 = 12. So the number hidden behind a butterfly is 4
